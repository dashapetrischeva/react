Homework №1

  • Task 1

    Solution

    Input the number of passengers. Calculate and display:

    • how many buses are required (each bus has 20 seats)
    • how many bottles of water are needed (2 bottles per passenger)
    • how many sandwiches are required (3 sandwiches per passenger)

  • Task 2

    Solution

    Input the month number. Automatically display the recommended clothing (winter – coat, summer – shorts, spring – sneakers, autumn – raincoat). Also, automatically display a corresponding image of the forest for that season.
